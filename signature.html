Email signature

Copy the signature below and paste it into Outlook. The phone numbers, email address and website stay as live links, so they work from a phone.

How to add it

Outlook keeps its own copy of whatever you paste, so if this page changes, the signature in Outlook does not. Clear the signature box first and paste again.

  1. Select everything between the two dashed lines — click just above the logo and drag to the end of the last line.
  2. Copy it (Cmd + C on a Mac, Ctrl + C on Windows).
  3. Outlook for Mac: Outlook menu → Settings → Signatures → +, then paste.
    Outlook on the web: gear icon → Mail → Compose and reply, then paste into the signature box.
  4. Set it as the default for new messages and for replies, and save.

Outlook underlines links in its own blue or black once pasted, and the Gmail app on a phone ignores the greys and sets every line in black. Both are the mail app’s own doing and cannot be overridden from a signature, so the ranking is carried by size, capitals and weight as well as colour — it still reads correctly when a client flattens it. The whole signature is 323px wide, which is inside a phone screen, so nothing has to wrap or shrink to fit.

If pasting gives you trouble

Use one or the other, not both. If the pasted version will not behave, clear the signature box completely and insert this picture instead: download the image. After inserting it, drag it down to about 430px wide — at its full 1400px the message becomes wider than a phone screen and everything past the left edge is cut off. The links on it are not clickable and it will not show at all for anyone whose email blocks images, which is why it is the fallback rather than the default.
