Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Who we are

This website is operated by L & M Highways Limited, trading as L&M Highways ("we", "us", "our"), a surfacing contractor registered in England and Wales under company number 15079120, with its registered office at Willow House, 46 St Andrews Street, Mildenhall, Bury St Edmunds, Suffolk, IP28 7HB. This policy explains how we collect, use and protect personal data when you use this website or contact us for a quote.

Information we collect

We may collect the following information when you use our enquiry form, call, or email us:

  • Your name and contact details (phone number, email address, postal address or site postcode)
  • Details of your enquiry, including project type and any information you choose to provide
  • Technical information such as IP address, browser type and pages visited, collected automatically when you browse the site

How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Respond to enquiries and provide quotations
  • Arrange and carry out site visits and surfacing works
  • Maintain records for accounting, insurance and legal purposes
  • Improve this website and our services

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

Legal basis for processing

We process your personal data on the basis of legitimate interest (responding to enquiries and delivering our services), contract (where you engage us for works), and consent (where you opt in to marketing communications, if applicable).

How long we keep your data

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy, including any legal, accounting or reporting requirements.

Sharing your information

We may share your information with trusted third parties who support our business operations (for example, accounting or IT service providers), and where required by law.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law (including the UK GDPR), you have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal data, and to object to its processing. To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below.

Cookies

This website does not set any cookies, and we do not use analytics, advertising or tracking services. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, and anything you send through a form on this site is processed by Web3Forms before it reaches us by email; as a normal part of serving those requests, both providers receive your IP address. If we later add anything that does set cookies, this section will be updated and a consent mechanism added at that point.

Contact us

If you have any questions about this policy or how your data is handled, please get in touch through our contact page, call us on 01638 493555, or write to us at Willow House, 46 St Andrews Street, Mildenhall, Bury St Edmunds, Suffolk, IP28 7HB.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "last updated" date.
