Our story

Built on commercial
standards, for every job.

L&M Highways is a professional surfacing contractor undertaking all aspects of asphalt paving — roads, car parks and surfaces of every type, for commercial and domestic clients alike.

Our staple has always been commercial contracting: car parks, access roads, industrial estates and highway works, where the margin for error is small and the standard expected is high. That's the standard we bring to every project — which is exactly why we're now expanding into domestic driveways and surfacing too.

Based in Mildenhall, near Bury St Edmunds, we now work nationwide — we're ready to travel anywhere for the right project. We bring commercial-grade plant, materials and workmanship to jobs of every size, across the country.

Accreditations & compliance

Carded, audited,
and site-ready.

The paperwork matters as much as the workmanship. Our accreditations mean main contractors can onboard us quickly, and every client knows exactly who is on their site.

Avetta registered

We're registered with Avetta, so main contractors can prequalify and onboard us through their existing supply chain system without hold-ups.

CSCS accredited

Every employee and operative we put on site holds a valid CSCS card — on commercial contracts and domestic jobs alike.

CPCS & NPORS plant operators

Our plant operators hold CPCS and NPORS accreditations for the machines they run, so every piece of plant on your site is in trained, certified hands.

Tier 1 experience

We work alongside main contractors and tier 1s on major projects — bringing the same standards, paperwork and reliability they expect.
