The project

Commercial standards,
domestic driveway.

A private driveway in Suffolk, built in two layers the way a road is — because a driveway that is only surfaced, rather than properly based, is the one that fails first.

The drive was taken up and the base prepared to the levels and falls the finished surface needed. Edgings and drainage were set first, so the new surface could be laid tight up to them rather than cut in afterwards.

It went down in two layers. A 50mm AC20 Dense binder course to carry the load, then 25mm of AC6 Dense Surf rolled to a closed finish. The fine aggregate in AC6 is what gives a domestic drive its smooth, even look, where a road surface is deliberately coarser for grip.

The detail is where a driveway is won or lost — the line against the drainage channel at the threshold, the chamber cover set flush rather than proud, the edge following the kerb cleanly. All of it hand laid, because no machine gets into those places.

Delivered on time, to the specification agreed, and within the budget the client had set out at the start.
