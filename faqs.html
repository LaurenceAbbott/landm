FAQs

Frequently asked
questions.

Everything you need to know before getting in touch. Can't find what you're after? Just ask us directly.

Do you work on both commercial and domestic projects?
Yes. Commercial surfacing has always been our staple — car parks, access roads and industrial estates — and we now apply that same standard of materials and workmanship to domestic driveways, patios and private roads.
What areas do you cover?
We're based in Mildenhall, near Bury St Edmunds, and cover Suffolk, Cambridgeshire and Norfolk — including Bury St Edmunds, Newmarket, Ely and Thetford and the surrounding areas. Get in touch if you're just outside this area; we're often able to help.
Is the quote free, and is it obligation-free?
Yes. We'll arrange a site visit to assess the job and provide a clear, written quotation at no cost and with no obligation to proceed.
How long does a driveway take to resurface?
It depends on the size and condition of the existing surface, but most domestic driveways are completed within one to two days, including groundworks and preparation. We'll confirm a timescale as part of your quote.
Do I need planning permission for a new driveway?
In most cases, no — but permission can be required depending on the size of the driveway, its permeability, and whether it fronts a classified road. We're happy to advise on your specific situation during the site visit.
What happens if it rains on the day of the works?
Surfacing work is weather-dependent, particularly for tarmac and resin. If conditions aren't suitable, we'll let you know in advance and reschedule at the earliest opportunity — we won't compromise on the quality of the finish.
Do you offer any guarantee on your work?
Yes — we stand behind our workmanship and materials. We'll confirm the specific guarantee for your project as part of your written quotation.
Can you match my quote to a fixed budget?
We're happy to discuss options that work within your budget, whether that means adjusting the specification, the area covered, or the finish. Let us know when you get in touch and we'll talk it through.
Do you work directly with businesses, or only through main contractors?
Both. We work directly with businesses, schools, councils and property managers, as well as alongside main contractors and developers on larger schemes.
